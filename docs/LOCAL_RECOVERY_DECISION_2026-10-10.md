## Recovery trigger and development consequence

A broader targeted lookup becomes useful when there is a named resident candidate, a separately available backup, a custody manifest containing a required hash, or a specific original cloud object. The partial search history and archive names alone do not provide that evidence. A full disk scan has not been performed.

The new public-data fixture should preserve the scientific intentions relevant to development—Watauga's gauge and catchment axis, explicit history and issue clocks, weather source identities, transformation rules and independent validation—while recording its new source and generator identities. Its outputs can qualify the new development recipe once validated; they cannot provide byte reproduction or reference-output agreement for the missing historical fixture. The manuscript should describe both evidence tracks and keep the historical reproduction result unresolved.
