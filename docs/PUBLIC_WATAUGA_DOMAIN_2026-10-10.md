# Public Watauga domain preparation — 10 October 2026

The owner's new-fixture request enabled an independently identified development input bundle. Public acquisition and generation have now produced a 31-catchment Watauga GeoPackage, 31 CFE configurations, 31 Noah namelists, a realization template, routing configuration, source-attribute table and retained MIT template notice. The [evidence receipt](evidence/public-watauga-domain-20261010.json) binds the source, adapter, templates and generated files. This does not restore any of the missing historical fixture members or establish a physical result. Original research folders were neither read nor written by this workstream.

The regional archive provided a small reproducible alternative to a national download. Data and dependencies remain under ignored project-local staging. The approved domain bounds were 48 MiB network acquisition, 64 MiB per archive, 128 MiB per extracted file and 256 MiB expanded domain staging.

## Gauge resolution and domain cut

The public hydrofabric's `gage` attribute associates USGS `03479000` with `wb-1017846`; taking its downstream nexus `nex-1017694` as the entire gauge basin would yield 32 catchments. Independent source review found a conflicting association: [USGS NLDI](https://api.water.usgs.gov/nldi/linked-data/nwissite/USGS-03479000) resolves the gauge to NHDPlus COMID `19743430`, which the acquired hydrofabric's network crosswalk maps to `wb-1017693`.

The [official station coordinates](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-03479000/) are approximately 56.92 m from the selected `wb-1017693` flowpath and 1,105.57 m from the attribute-associated `wb-1017846`. The exact point lies in an adjacent tributary polygon near the junction, so nearest polygon or nearest line alone is insufficient. The selected 31-catchment domain uses the NLDI COMID crosswalk and upstream closure **inclusive of `wb-1017693`**. Its polygon area is 237.865509 km², compared with the station's published 92.1 mi² (238.537906 km²). The discrepancy and conflicting source attributes are retained.

The selected gauge output is routed flowpath `wb-1017693`. Its downstream terminal nexus is `nex-1017694`; the downstream flowpath lies outside the extracted domain, and the adjacent tributary `wb-1017846` is excluded. A junction sum at that terminal nexus would include an inappropriate additional contribution. Using the full selected reach as the interior station's surrogate, without segment splitting, remains a spatial limitation. None of these identifiers is silently equated with the historical sealed routing identity.

The adapter treats non-null network nodes as vertices and deduplicates repeated edges. Null-ID rows cannot join unrelated rivers; cyclic or conflicting downstream relationships fail. Catchment geometry and attribute axes must match the derived closure exactly. Actual checks found SQLite integrity `ok`, all 31 polygons valid and nonempty, and summed polygon area equal to union area within floating-point precision. The resulting 253,952-byte GeoPackage hashes to `76edff60e02c074f0e28e84ca33ab4f22cead829f748322598d5cad2e8a1a123`.

## Configuration generation and qualification

[`prepare_domain.py`](../scripts/public_fixture/prepare_domain.py) is independently authored. It opens the bound public GeoPackage read-only and immutable, writes only into a fresh repository-local `.local` directory, and does not import upstream or inherited generators. Inputs use no-follow resident-file checks; source bytes are checked before and after preparation. Source triggers and extensions are not copied or executed. The reduced GeoPackage retains selected table rows, geometry and CRS metadata; national spatial extents are cleared rather than represented as subset extents.

External templates are bound to [NGIAB preprocessing commit `2b41ba2`](https://github.com/CIROH-UA/NGIAB_data_preprocess/tree/2b41ba2f038445ddbfe01d8cbe266e2aa9b5e635). The pinned repository license is MIT, and its notice is retained alongside generated files. CFE bottom drainage uses `mean.slope_1km` directly. Noah terrain slope uses the separate upstream expression `degrees(arctan(tan(radians(mean.slope))/100))`; neither field is substituted for the other or clipped. Null/nonfinite source values and selected range/class violations fail. The source GeoPackage does not authoritatively establish these fields' units and derivation, so that qualification remains open.

The new recipe declares cold initial CFE groundwater and soil storage ratios of 0.05 and retains the pinned Noah initialization/options. It does not borrow a historical land state. The parent preparation interval is 1 December 2021 00:00 UTC through 2 January 2022 18:00 UTC exclusive, or 786 provider hours. Routing is configured at 300 seconds with two workers, channel routing, and disabled streamflow/reservoir assimilation. These are inspectable development settings, not a calibrated or approved scientific default.

The final domain preparation snapshot is privately retained as `public-fixture-final` (local `fixture-v2`), with preparation identity `81244db9c23c141da82fd684e6829c80044a2a0c2d619f3976805e358353a4ac`. It binds 67 generated files totaling 423,976 bytes, plus its separately written preparation receipt. Earlier generation snapshots remain separate. Their geometry bytes are identical.

The domain snapshot's realization retains a NetCDF template path, which cannot directly consume the separately prepared provider CSV files. [`assemble_native.py`](../scripts/public_fixture/assemble_native.py) provides a distinct assembly revision based on the [pinned NextGen CSV provider](https://github.com/CIROH-UA/ngen/blob/9175b640a4e6f1d90e58e0a65662174d08f052ef/include/forcing/CsvPerFeatureForcingProvider.hpp), [provider selector](https://github.com/CIROH-UA/ngen/blob/9175b640a4e6f1d90e58e0a65662174d08f052ef/include/realizations/catchment/Formulation_Constructors.hpp) and [simulation clock](https://github.com/CIROH-UA/ngen/blob/9175b640a4e6f1d90e58e0a65662174d08f052ef/include/simulation_time/Simulation_Time.hpp). It selects `CsvPerFeature`, a per-catchment filename pattern and space-separated UTC timestamps. NextGen's end label is inclusive: the last provider label is one hour before the physical exclusive stop. The assembly sets that label explicitly, retains a separate Noah physical endpoint and computes 12 routing steps per provider hour.

Actual [assembly evidence](evidence/public-watauga-native-assembly-20261010.json) now records three generated jobs against the sealed final public weather bundle: a history-only job with 786 provider hours per catchment; an independent 1 January 23:00 issue replay with 767 historical plus 18 forecast hours; and a 2 January 00:00 issue replay with 768 historical plus 18 forecast hours. Each starts at the declared origin. The 291 bound generated files total 12,247,061 bytes. All 93 catchment CSVs have the exact selected header, clock coverage and final provider label; 167 bound source assets were reverified after assembly. The final assembly identity is `c650369eb86228ea5961b8fef9e0898f4ad257653f8a795088c7234d8332b959`. The original domain snapshot and earlier assembly revision remain retained separately.

This establishes an inspectable CSV input layout. Native execution, shared-history branching, complete land-state recovery and physical comparison have not occurred. Static parameter tables and loaded-library/component paths remain open qualification steps, so the native input layout is not a runnable-profile admission.

Thirteen domain tests and six assembly tests passed using synthetic bytes, including domain cuts, malformed topology, source/template corruption, clock gaps, duplicate timestamps, nonfinite values, no-overwrite behavior and protected destination refusal. They do not establish forecast accuracy. Noah parameter tables, loaded libraries and component compatibility, source units, weather interpretation, endpoint behavior and the routing cut still require qualification; all reports retain `execution_ready=false`.

The related Lynker v2.2 license states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for that bucket's paths. Its applicability to the distinct `communityhydrofabric` archive is unresolved. Third-party payloads and templates remain private staging rather than public repository contents; the public artifacts here are the new adapter, tests and provenance summaries.
