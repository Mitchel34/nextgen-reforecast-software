## Scientific and operational boundaries

Forcing validation consumes bounded decoded CSVs and strict manifests. It does not authenticate the upstream source, reconstruct historical availability or qualify unresolved radiation conventions. Gap handling defaults to failure. An explicit exclusion can acknowledge an incomplete forecast issue, never replace its weather or skip initialization history. All 18 forecast source leads remain required regardless of retained output selection.

CFE validation audits a particular drainage mapping against actual bound source/configuration/library bytes. It does not validate the complete model configuration or attest a dynamically loaded library. Historical `mean.slope` reproduction remains diagnostic; a candidate `mean.slope_1km` experiment needs separate identity, initialization and coupled qualification. Unknown source units remain unknown.

Original Reforecast, NextGen, HYDRA and thesis directories remain read-only, with no hydration. Protected metadata and immutable repository bytes are compared before and after work. Exact source copies and acquired binaries stay in ignored private staging. No inherited implementation, scientific data or binaries enter the public commit. Public source references and sanitized identity/evidence records are published separately.

The dedicated local ARM64 worker uses two CPUs and 8 GiB RAM, with no original-folder mounts or active Docker-context switch. Actual aggregate runtime staging/cache/disk usage across project staging and the dedicated short worker directory is limited to 12 GiB; host free space must remain at least 8 GiB. Compressed downloads are capped at 4 GiB within that aggregate budget. Existing unrelated Colima profiles are untouched. The isolated exported-runtime inspection uses no network, a read-only container root, dropped capabilities, a small tmpfs and a 60-second/256-MiB bound. It lists ELF dependencies without invoking the model entry point.

No model simulation, native compilation, paid service, recruitment or submission follows merely from a passed byte or content check. Native build/physical execution still requires its complete admitted recipe and assets. A newly built runtime would receive its own identity and qualification; it cannot inherit historical binary evidence.
