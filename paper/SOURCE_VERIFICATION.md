## CFE mapping and inspected prior isolated sensitivity

The owner initially reported an isolated synthetic-weather sensitivity and then supplied its existing evidence bundle. The review team inspected resident documentation, driver/source and execution receipts; the coordinator recomputed paired-result statistics and another reviewer independently checked the raw run-summary CSV, saved configuration controls and source hashes. The [sanitized review receipt](../docs/evidence/cfe-sensitivity-review.json) binds the inspected artifacts by SHA-256 and records that CFE was not rerun. The paired-result CSV has SHA-256 `56c3d95796a5bd51a93656013f3ebbdc90433d8519629b0b9f2451a7517b3d0c`; the source bundle is not publicly deposited. This manuscript lane read the sanitized receipt and coordinator's verified driver interpretation, without opening the protected original evidence directory.

The prior experiment uses CFE source `f9182dfb3d81c407c66c5b782114173893c30cf2`, not an attested historical deployed library. The driver changes `slop` before fresh initialization for each variant/scenario, uses a weekly 30-mm six-hour pulse and 0.1-mm/h potential evaporation, warms up for 17,472 hours and evaluates for 8,736 hours. Its first-18-hour runoff-depth measure sums `flux_Qout_m * 1000` at evaluation onset, simulation hours 17,472–17,489. Noah coupling, routing and observations are absent.

For 31 Watauga catchments, recomputation of `100 * (inherited / candidate - 1)` yields an unweighted median of `102.83795727904703%` in that 18-hour window (range `20.155931898074986–220.14810720716068%`) and `2.4847718183552248%` across the full evaluation (range `2.114465383626851–4.185696425082774%`). All 31 pairs are valid; none are excluded. These are independently recomputed arithmetic summaries of saved prior outputs, not an independently reproduced simulation or a new public-runtime experiment. The window is at synthetic storm onset, not averaged across all issues. Neither statistic establishes basin runoff, gauge discharge, forecast skill, a coupled-model response or a full-reforecast effect; the full-window value is not a universal long-term bound.

The upstream mapping change is independently verified; saved Watauga configurations and variants were checked against the isolated-experiment manifest, while the original hydrofabric extraction and historical generating/library provenance remain separate unresolved questions. The [finding record](../docs/CFE_PARAMETER_FINDING.md) and [pinned upstream record](../docs/evidence/ngiab-pr74.json) distinguish those scopes and define next qualification steps. A direct web open of the individual change-commit page was restricted, but the complete official PR diff was readable. Original configurations and the historical dossier were not changed. Only the manuscript, claims register and this verification note were edited by this lane; no model or sensitivity experiment was executed during this review.
