## Adversarial review and evidence inventory

The integration review should test changed installed implementation/runtime/input/reference bytes, mismatched issue or origin, unsupported domains/locations, wrong forecast clocks, exhausted allowances and ambiguous lock types before launch. Source routing feature IDs and campaign reach IDs must map explicitly without inventing another outlet or dropping their original identifiers. After a launch, zero exit with empty/partial output, fatal logs, incomplete native/nexus/routing axes, nonfinite discharge and mismatched executed identity must remain failed attempts. Every process attempt counts even if its output is rejected.

Archive review should reject corrupt or incompatible existing commits, duplicate/missing keys, collapsed forecasts sharing valid time, wrong lead projections and substituted receipts. Interrupted staging must never expose a partial completed issue. Verification is fresh at use time; a previous successful hash check cannot establish unchanged current bytes. Preserve both all 18 generated leads and the selected product, with their distinct denominators.

The handoff should include source/version identities, exact commands and environments, targeted adversarial results, installed-wheel checks, all actual process receipts, full-array/reference comparisons, campaign commit identity and complete issue keys, status/verification output and export round-trip evidence. Keep offline/synthetic tests, newly executed diagnostic outcomes, historical evidence and independent UX/operator studies in separate categories.
