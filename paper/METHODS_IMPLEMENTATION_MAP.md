## Formal experiment interpretation

Historical E03 remains anchored to its original historical origin, geometry, physical parameters, component/runtime identities and historical forcing. The separately authorized [public Watauga fixture](../docs/PUBLIC_WATAUGA_FIXTURE_PLAN.md) prepares new source-bound inputs without replacing E03 or inheriting its results. Any physical reference for those inputs requires a separate prospective protocol and allowance. For issue \(I\), the historical parent has consumed \([H,I)\); forecast provider labels are \(I\) through \(I+17\mathrm{h}\), and the generated target times are \(I+1\mathrm{h}\) through \(I+18\mathrm{h}\). A request to retain leads 1–6 selects targets; it does not authorize omitting the last twelve physical updates or their forcing inputs.

Request normalization can map an explicit UTC cadence or an issue list to the same canonical schedule. Equivalence of schedules is a planner statement; it does not establish that the source archive contains every requested issue. Similarly, output selection changes a campaign/product identity while the underlying antecedent physical-state inputs may remain the same. The planner's domain input identity and the planning lock's per-domain provenance identity serve different scopes: the latter binds the wider campaign context, including issue schedule, retained selection, resources and declared metadata. It is not a demonstrated key for reusing a physical trajectory. Scientific metadata fields remain null when unknown or explicitly declared-unvalidated when supplied. A lightweight planning lock cannot certify the complete scientific state or make an unavailable model ready to run.

The browser's date labels must state whether they describe issue times or forecast-valid times and show the final target extending beyond the last issue. Prototype choices should be preserved as versioned request behavior and evaluated before they become promises about live source coverage. No-forecast simulation, arbitrary domains or horizons beyond the admitted model profile remain separately scoped decisions.

## Results and writing discipline

The public software results section can report tested offline contracts with their exact environment and revision. It cannot use these checks to fill Q1–Q4's physical comparisons or Q5's independent/researcher outcomes. Internal browser QA is not a conducted UX_E1 study: a study requires its declared manifest, assignments, participants/roles, attempts and evidence-level interpretation. A successful local preview is not a hosted deployment.

For the initial diagnostic physical work, admit the original mapping concern explicitly, then generate and seal the two independent references before consuming them in E03 comparisons. Retain the original benchmark IDs, bounds and thresholds. A corrected parameter profile receives a separate scientific identity, antecedent history and applicable qualification. Neither the historical zero-difference receipt nor the prior isolated CFE arithmetic establishes that corrected coupled result.

Use the related-work guide as an organizational aid: R3/R6/R7 for the formal contract and comparison purposes, R1/R2/R4 for workflow-to-architecture explanation, and R5/R8 for transparent implementation and user-evaluation evidence. No new external scientific claim or journal requirement is introduced by this map. The manuscript retains its evidence register, including C46 for the separately identified public-data preparation; physical results, user results, final licensing, artifact deposits and author declarations remain pending.
