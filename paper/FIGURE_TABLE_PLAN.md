An optional supplementary historical table can reproduce narrowly selected dossier values with receipt SHA-256, binary identity, resource allocation and raw-array recheck status. It must not be merged into new benchmark results. Include source/treatment details, source-decoder qualification, units and additional adverse tests in the supplement rather than obscuring them in a caption.

If the CFE sensitivity receives a figure, source it from the saved isolated-experiment data with permissions and hashes, label its current-source CFE version and synthetic forcing, and preserve the distinction between first-18-hour storm-onset and full-52-week endpoints. Do not depict it as a coupled or corrected-reforecast result. A future coupled comparison requires its own independently evolved parameter variants and new results. Do not fabricate a 20-hour/two-issue data panel: that smaller real fixture is proposed, not available.

Use standard plotting libraries to produce vector PDF/SVG plus accessible raster previews where useful. Record plot-script commit and input receipt hashes. Axis labels carry units; color encodes method consistently and is supplemented with line styles/markers. Avoid a map until redistributable geometry is available. The final number of main figures/tables remains subject to the verified journal guide.
