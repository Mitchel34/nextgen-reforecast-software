## Verified publisher guidance with limited scope

Elsevier's official [Highlights support page](https://www.elsevier.support/publishing/answer/how-do-i-include-highlights-with-my-manuscript), updated 1 June 2026 and read during this review, describes 3–5 items with at most 85 characters each, normally in a separate editable file. It explicitly directs authors to the journal guide to determine whether highlights are required. This is general publisher guidance only. We have not created result-bearing highlights because new results are pending.

The [NGIAB article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envsoft.2025.106666) and publisher-deposited Crossref metadata confirm that a related software article appeared in *Environmental Modelling & Software*, volume 193, article 106666 (2025). That precedent supports the proposed topical direction; it does not establish editorial acceptance, novelty, present requirements or suitability of this unfinished draft.

## Final preparation procedure

After the guide is accessible, record access date and exact requirements with supporting links. Check the completed text and every uploaded file against those requirements. Confirm authorship, contributions, institutions, funding and interests with the actual authors. Replace proposed result statements only from retained experiments. Freeze the code/data/image versions used by the manuscript and verify their public access. Journal submission and any messages to editors require a separate explicit instruction; nothing in this file submits or contacts anyone.
